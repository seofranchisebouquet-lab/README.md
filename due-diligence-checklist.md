# Food Franchise Due Diligence Checklist

Research is essential before investing in any food franchise.

## Checklist

- [ ] Verify the total investment requirement
- [ ] Understand the franchise fee
- [ ] Check royalty and marketing charges
- [ ] Review the franchise agreement
- [ ] Understand renewal and termination conditions
- [ ] Check territory or location rights
- [ ] Understand supplier requirements
- [ ] Review training and operational support
- [ ] Research existing outlets
- [ ] Compare local competitors
- [ ] Calculate expected operating expenses
- [ ] Estimate working-capital requirements
- [ ] Independently verify financial projections

## Final Consideration

A franchise <a href="(https://www.franchisebouquet.com/)"> should be evaluated on its actual unit economics, market demand, location suitability, operating requirements, and contractual obligations.

Information found online should be treated as a starting point. Important financial and legal information should be confirmed directly with the relevant franchisor and qualified professionals where appropriate.
