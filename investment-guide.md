# Food Franchise Investment Guide

The investment required for a food franchise varies according to the brand, city, outlet format, property size, equipment, and business model.

## Common Cost Components

- Franchise or licensing fee
- Interior and outlet setup
- Kitchen equipment
- Security deposit and rent
- Initial inventory
- Staff salaries
- POS and technology
- Marketing expenses
- Packaging
- Working capital

## Working Capital

Working capital is important because a new outlet may require time to reach stable sales. Entrepreneurs should maintain sufficient funds for regular expenses during the initial operating period.

## Financial Evaluation

Instead of looking only at the franchise fee, calculate:

**Total Investment = Setup Costs + Initial Expenses + Working Capital**

Also evaluate expected sales, food costs, labour costs, rent, royalties, marketing contributions, and other recurring expenses.

Projected returns should be treated as estimates and independently verified.
