# Location and Market Research

Location is one of the most important factors when evaluating Food Franchise Opportunities in India.

A strong brand may still struggle if the outlet is placed in an unsuitable market.

## Factors to Research

### Customer Profile
Understand the age group, income level, lifestyle, and food preferences of potential customers.

### Footfall
Check pedestrian and vehicle movement during different times of the day and across weekdays and weekends.

### Competition
Identify direct competitors as well as alternative food businesses in the same catchment area.

### Rent
Compare expected sales potential with rent and other property-related costs.

### Accessibility
Parking, visibility, public transport, road access, and delivery reach can influence customer convenience.

## Market Validation

Before signing a property agreement, conduct local research instead of relying only on general market trends.

A location should support the franchise's target customer, pricing, menu, and operating format.
